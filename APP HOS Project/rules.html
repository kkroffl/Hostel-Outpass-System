Rules & Regulations

Please read and follow these guidelines when requesting an outpass. These rules ensure smooth operations and safety for all hostel residents.

1. Submit accurate information

All students must provide correct details in outpass requests.

2. Respect timings

Students should return at the time specified in the outpass.

3. Emergency contacts

Provide valid emergency numbers for quick communication.

Important Notice

• All students must comply with these rules to maintain their outpass privileges.

• Violation may result in disciplinary action including suspension of outpass facility.

• For emergencies, contact the hostel office immediately at +91 12345 67890.

• Rules are subject to change based on administration decisions.
